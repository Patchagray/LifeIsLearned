# Original starter illustrations

Created using the built-in image generation capability for this project. Images are bundled at:

- LifeIsLearned/Resources/Assets.xcassets/priors-setup.imageset/illustration.png
- LifeIsLearned/Resources/Assets.xcassets/priors-conflict.imageset/illustration.png
- LifeIsLearned/Resources/Assets.xcassets/priors-resolution.imageset/illustration.png

Prompt basis: landscape 3:2 editorial story illustrations, ink outlines, muted teal/amber/cream palette, paper texture, a sunlit college meeting room. Malik: Black young adult, short curls, mustard overshirt. Elena: Latina young adult, dark wavy shoulder-length hair, sage sweater. No text, logos, mascot, or app UI.

Scene prompts:
1. Setup: two student representatives share a laptop, studying thoughtfully. Very subtle reflections suggest Malik's positive work experience and Elena's memory of an exhausted student.
2. Disagreement: Malik points at a teal rising chart and Elena at an amber caution symbol, politely disputing the same mixed report. Concerned, attentive faces; no shouting.
3. Collaboration: both jointly examine one report with benefits and cautions, listening and revising their view together.

Illustrations are narrative art, not a data visualization. The abstract charts must never be interpreted as real research results. Character rendering may vary slightly between scenes.
