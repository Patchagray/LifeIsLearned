# Handoff 006 physical-device checklist

Status: pending manual checks on the H006 build. Simulator tests are recorded separately. No personal device library was reset or replaced during H006 development.

- Open the app repeatedly with the existing library. Confirm installed books, earned cards, favorites, history and reading positions remain present, with no recovery banner.
- Switch Your Library / Explore; confirm the home hero, Ideas and Settings remain and searches/counts do not mix. Check iPhone and iPad layouts, landscape and large text. Use VoiceOver to select both modes, shelves, details and action buttons.
- Confirm + offers Scan a Book and Import File; cancel/dismiss returns to the same screen.
- Scan an ISBN or cover. Review a match, open focused Explore, then return to all books. Confirm camera frames stay on device and Request still requires an explicit tap.
- For an approved published release, start/cancel/retry a download, install it, update it and confirm removed ideas still require review. Turn off networking and reopen an installed book.
- Offload a remote book, confirm reclaimed bytes, use Restore in Your Library or History, and check cards/progress/favorites remain after redownload. A manually imported offloaded book must offer Re-import book.
- Read an installed narrated idea offline. Check packaged-audio pause/resume, manual navigation, background/screen lock, lock-screen Play/Pause, interruptions, Story following and practice boundaries. For a book without valid packaged audio, verify device fallback voices.

Real-network catalog/package and restore checks require owner-approved public assets first. Synthetic simulator fixtures are not public books or evidence of production voice quality.
