## Explicit boundaries

The full suite retains two intentional simulator skips per device: hardware premium-voice measurement and the older system-setting-dependent Reduce Motion UI check. New completion reduced-motion rendering and reader policy checks run deterministically. Hardware voices, haptics, silent-switch behavior, camera recognition and before/after device artwork review remain separate checks. Production release URLs/backend provisioning and real-network smoke are not replaced by URLProtocol fixtures.
